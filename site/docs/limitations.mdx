## Roadmap

dnd's first set of capabilities is complete: draggable, droppable, sortable and swappable, each
its own entry. What comes next:

1. **Publishing**, once the maintainer has validated the library: the packages on npm, a
   changelog and these pages on fragiola.com.
2. **Other adapters**: the core is framework-free so that Vue, Angular and others can follow
   React.

## Not planned

- **Sorting:** virtualised sortable lists, multi-select sorting, nested sortable trees and sorting
  across windows.
- **Motion:** built-in drop animations. The shifts' transitions are your CSS
  ([animating shifts](/docs/guides/animating-shifts)).
- **Modifiers** beyond auto-scroll (axis lock, snap to grid, restrict to a container), multi-select
  drags and drags across windows.

Shipped CSS, icons, text, a mandatory provider and HTML5 drag and drop as the move mechanism are
out by design.

## Known limits

- **The drag sources are larger than their targets.** Over the shared entry, `/draggable` adds
  +4.6 KB min + gzip (target +2 KB), `/sortable` +7.4 KB (target +2.5 KB) and `/swappable`
  +5.4 KB (target +1.5 KB). They share one drag-source machinery, paid once: see
  [bundle size](/docs/guides/bundle-size#sortable-and-swappable).
- **Keyboard moves do not scroll the destination into view.** Moving a sortable item or a
  draggable by keyboard to a slot or a droppable outside its scroll container's visible area does
  not scroll there; auto-scroll follows the pointer only.
- **The grid strategy suits items of one size.** An item moved in a `grid` takes its new place's
  corner; items of varied sizes keep their order but may not line up. Lists (`verticalList`,
  `horizontalList`) take items of any size.
- **A group name is a droppable id during a sort.** It shares its scope's droppable id space:
  keep group names and droppable ids distinct.
- **A drag never crosses documents.** A drag inside a Dockable popout stays in that window, and a
  drag cannot carry an item from the main window into a popout.
- **Auto-scroll uses the scroll range measured when the drag starts.** Content that grows during
  the drag (a list that loads more) is not scrolled into until the next drag.
- **An `onMouseDown` on a draggable does not run.** The press is claimed on `pointerdown`, which
  suppresses the compatibility mouse events; use `onPointerDown`. `click` still fires.
- **Firefox scroll anchoring.** In a scroll container, Firefox may take a dragged or shifted
  element as its scroll anchor, and scroll the container back by as much when the core clears its
  `translate`. The packages ship no CSS, so set `overflow-anchor: none` on the container yourself
  (the auto-scroll and sortable examples do).
- **Grid Layout integrations are local only** until Grid Layout is on npm: see
  [with Grid Layout](/docs/guides/with-grid-layout).
